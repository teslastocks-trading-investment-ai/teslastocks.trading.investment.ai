SpaceX is a revolutionary space technology company founded by Elon Musk in 2002. Neuralink is a neurotechnology company founded by Elon Musk in 2016, aiming to develop implantable brain-computer interfaces (BCIs) that enable people to control devices with their thoughts. The company's primary goal is to help individuals with paralysis or other motor disorders regain lost communication skills and mobility.
