    Philosophy

    Tesla's philosophy is centered around accelerating the world's transition to sustainable energy, with a focus on innovation, disruption, and environmental stewardship. Founded by Elon Musk, the company aims to lead the electric vehicle revolution and promote renewable energy solutions, such as solar power and energy storage. By pushing the boundaries of technology and design, Tesla seeks to create a cleaner, more efficient, and more sustainable future for humanity.

    History

    Tesla was founded in 2003 by Martin Eberhard and Marc Tarpenning, who aimed to prove that electric vehicles could be better than gasoline-powered cars. Elon Musk, who had previously co-founded PayPal, led the company's Series A funding round in 2004 and became chairman of the board. In 2008, Musk took over as CEO and led the company's efforts to develop its first car, the Tesla Roadster.

    Culture

    Tesla's culture is characterized by innovation, disruption, and a relentless pursuit of sustainability. The company's headquarters, designed by Elon Musk, resembles a futuristic spaceship, reflecting its forward-thinking approach. Tesla's employees, known as "Tesla Team Members," are encouraged to think outside the box, challenge conventional norms, and strive for perfection. The company's emphasis on collaboration, creativity, and continuous learning has created a dynamic and inclusive work environment that attracts top talent from around the world.

    Trust the Professionals

    At Tesla, "Trust the Professionals" reflects the company's confidence in its team of experts who design, engineer, and manufacture electric vehicles and clean energy products. With a focus on engineering excellence, innovative spirit, quality, and safety, Tesla's professionals are dedicated to pushing the boundaries of what's possible. From autonomous driving and battery technology to manufacturing and production, Tesla's team of experts is committed to delivering innovative products and solutions that are transforming the way we think about transportation and energy.

    Trading instruments

    In Tesla, trading instruments are not directly related to the company's primary business of electric vehicle manufacturing and clean energy solutions. However, Tesla's stock (TSLA) is a popular trading instrument among investors and traders, with many using technical and fundamental analysis to buy and sell shares. Additionally, options and futures contracts on Tesla stock are also available, allowing traders to speculate on price movements or hedge existing positions.

    Countries covered

    esla has a significant presence in several countries around the world. The company has established manufacturing facilities in the United States (Fremont, California, and Austin, Texas), China (Shanghai), and is expanding its global footprint with new factories in Germany (Berlin) and Texas (Austin). Tesla vehicles are sold in over 50 countries, including the United States, China, Canada, Europe, Australia, and Japan, with a growing network of retail stores, service centers, and Supercharger stations to support its global customer base.
